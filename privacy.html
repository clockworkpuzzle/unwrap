隱私權政策

拆禮物 Unwrap · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

拆禮物（以下稱「本遊戲」）由個人開發者製作與營運。這份政策說明本遊戲會處理哪些資料、用在哪裡，以及你可以怎麼控制。

1. 不需要帳號

本遊戲不需要註冊或登入，我們不會要求或收集你的姓名、電子郵件、電話等個人資料。

2. 存在你手機上的資料

遊戲進度、生命、道具數量、每日挑戰紀錄與設定都只存在你的裝置上，不會上傳到我們的伺服器。刪除本遊戲就會一起刪除。

3. 廣告（Google AdMob 與聯播夥伴）

本遊戲只靠廣告營運，沒有任何付費內容。廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，也可能透過 AdMob 的聯播合作夥伴（AppLovin、Unity Ads、Pangle、LINE）提供。為了顯示與衡量廣告，這些服務可能會收集：

  • 裝置的廣告識別碼（IDFA）：只有在你於系統詢問時選擇「允許追蹤」才會取得
  • IP 位址、裝置型號、系統版本、語言
  • 廣告的顯示與點擊紀錄

你沒有允許追蹤時，仍會看到廣告，但不會依你的跨 App 活動投放。位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士的玩家，第一次開啟時會看到同意視窗，之後可以在遊戲「設定 → 廣告隱私設定」修改。Google 如何使用資料：policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. 數據分析（Google Firebase）

本遊戲用 Firebase Analytics 與 Remote Config 了解關卡是否太難、哪裡出問題，並遠端調整遊戲數值。會收集：

  • 遊玩紀錄：開始、完成或放棄的關卡、花費時間、使用道具次數、看廣告等事件
  • App 的隨機識別碼、裝置型號、系統版本、大略地區（國家／城市層級）

這些資料不包含你的姓名或聯絡方式，只用來改善遊戲。Firebase 的隱私說明：firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. 通知

生命回滿、每日禮物的提醒是在你手機上排程的本地通知，不經過我們的伺服器。可以在遊戲「設定」或 iPhone「設定 → 通知」關閉。

6. Game Center

排行榜由 Apple Game Center 提供（如果你有登入），會使用你在 Game Center 的暱稱與成績，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。

7. 兒童

本遊戲適合所有年齡遊玩，但不是專為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們不會故意收集兒童的個人資料。如果你認為孩子的資料被收集了，請聯絡我們。

8. 你可以怎麼做

  • 關閉追蹤：iPhone「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」
  • 刪除手機上的遊戲資料：刪除本遊戲
  • 要求刪除分析資料或有任何疑問：寫信給我們，請附上大約的遊玩時間與裝置型號

9. 政策更新

政策有變更時會更新這個頁面，並修改上方的生效日期。

10. 聯絡我們

faststudio.bus@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Unwrap! · Effective November 24, 2026

Unwrap! (“the game”) is made and run by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the game handles, why, and how you can control it.

1. No account needed

The game has no sign-up or log-in. We never ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or similar personal details.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress, lives, items, daily challenge records and settings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers. Deleting the game deletes them.

3. Ads (Google AdMob and mediation partners)

The game is supported only by ads and has no paid content. Ads are served by Google AdMob and may also come from AdMob mediation partners (AppLovin, Unity Ads, Pangle, LINE). To show and measure ads, these services may collect:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you choose “Allow” when iOS asks about tracking
  • IP address, device model, OS version and language
  • Records of ads shown and tapped

If you don’t allow tracking you’ll still see ads, but they won’t be based on your activity across other apps. Players in the EEA, UK or Switzerland see a consent form on first launch and can change their choice later in the game under Settings → Ad privacy. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Analytics (Google Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Remote Config to learn whether levels are too hard, find problems, and tune game settings remotely. It collects:

  • Gameplay events: levels started, cleared or abandoned, time taken, items used, ads watched
  • A random app instance ID, device model, OS version and approximate location (country/city level)

This data doesn’t include your name or contact details and is used only to improve the game. Firebase privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Notifications

Reminders for refilled lives and daily gifts are local notifications scheduled on your phone; they don’t go through our servers. Turn them off in the game’s Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

6. Game Center

Leaderboards are provided by Apple Game Center (if you’re signed in) and use your Game Center nickname and scores under Apple’s privacy policy.

7. Children

The game is suitable for all ages but isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child’s data has been collected, please contact us.

8. Your choices

  • Turn off tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Delete game data on your phone: delete the game
  • Ask us to delete analytics data, or any other question: email us with roughly when you played and your device model

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.

10. Contact

faststudio.bus@gmail.com
